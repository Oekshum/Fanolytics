The 7 new atoms this mod adds, collectively referred to as the Fanolytics, each correspond to a point on the Fano Plane.
The Glyph of Reflection takes a fanolytic in the hole, "reflects" it across another, onto the third point of their shared line. 
  For example: Knuth, Klein, and Sanders are on a line in the Fano Plane. If you put Klein in the bowl of the glyph, and then put Sanders in the hole, you get Knuth out of the Iris.
